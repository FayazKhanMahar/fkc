Study in China

A guide for Pakistani students to the major fully-funded scholarships in China — for Bachelor's, Master's and PhD — and how to apply.

Why China

One of the world's largest scholarship systems

Huge funding pool

The Chinese government funds tens of thousands of international scholarships every year, across hundreds of universities.

Familiar for Pakistanis

Tens of thousands of Pakistani students already study in China, with halal food, strong ties, and easy travel.

Rising universities

Chinese universities are climbing global rankings fast, with strong research facilities and modern campuses.

The main options

Major scholarships to know

There are several routes to funded study in China. These are the big ones.

🎓 Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)

The flagship program, funded by China's Ministry of Education and offered at 270+ universities for Bachelor's, Master's and PhD.

  • Full tuition
  • Free dormitory or accommodation support
  • Monthly stipend (≈ CNY 2,500–3,500 by level)
  • Health insurance
  • Airfare/travel not covered

Pakistani students usually apply through HEC (the official nominating body), as well as the CSC portal.

🀄 Confucius Institute Scholarship (CIS)

A fully-funded scholarship focused on Chinese language and culture, offered at 150+ universities.

  • Tuition and accommodation
  • Monthly living allowance
  • Medical insurance
  • Usually requires an HSK (Chinese proficiency) score
  • Applied for via a recommending Confucius Institute

🏙️ Provincial government scholarships

Many Chinese provinces run their own schemes (e.g. Jiangsu, Shanghai, Beijing). These are often overlooked — and therefore less competitive. Applied for through the university after you receive admission.

🏫 University scholarships

Top universities like Tsinghua, Fudan and Zhejiang offer their own merit-based scholarships that can fully fund studies — sometimes without going through CSC at all.

Who can apply

General eligibility

Exact criteria vary by program and university, but in general:

Nationality & health

Be a non-Chinese national (Pakistani / AJK for the HEC route) in good health.

Age guidelines

Commonly under 25 for Bachelor's, under 35 for Master's, and under 40 for PhD.

Language

English (IELTS/TOEFL) or Chinese (HSK), depending on the program's language of instruction.

Get ready

Documents you'll typically need

Passport

Educational records (attested)

Transcripts

Language proof (IELTS/TOEFL or HSK)

Recommendation letters

Study plan / research proposal

Foreigner Physical Examination Form (medical)

Passport photo

Pre-admission/supervisor letter (helps a lot)

The journey

How the application works

01

Pick scholarship & route

Decide between the HEC route, a university route, or a language scholarship.

02

Shortlist & contact

Choose universities and programs; for Master's/PhD, contacting a supervisor early helps.

03

Apply on the portals

Submit on the required portals (e.g. HEC and CSC) with all documents before the deadline.

04

Selection & visa

If selected, receive your admission and JW202 form, then apply for your student (X) visa.

Timing

When to apply

Application windows generally run from around December/January to March/April, with results by mid-year. Important: for Pakistani students, the HEC route deadline is usually earlier (around January) than the general university deadlines.

Always confirm the current year's dates. Deadlines, seat numbers, stipend amounts and portal rules change every year. Check the official sources below for the exact, up-to-date call before you apply — or ask us and we'll confirm for you.
