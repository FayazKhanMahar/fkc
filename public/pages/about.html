About Fayaz Khan Consultancy

We started with one belief: a student's potential — not their postcode or their budget — should decide how far they go.

Our story

Built by someone who walked the road first

Applying abroad is confusing, expensive-looking, and full of dead ends — endless portals, unclear funding, and advice that's either generic or trying to sell you something.

Fayaz Khan Consultancy exists to fix that. We give students the kind of guidance we wished we'd had: honest, specific, and focused on funded admissions to strong universities — for both Master's (ME) and Ph.D. applicants.

No false promises. No copy-paste applications. Just one advisor who learns your file and stays with you from the first email to the day you land.
